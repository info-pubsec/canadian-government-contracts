# Canadian Government Contracts — The Complete Guide

Canada buys $200B+ a year and posts it all publicly — across two dozen portals. This guide maps the entire landscape: federal, provincial, and municipal, with competition data showing where you can actually win.

## The landscape

| Level | Portal | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Federal | CanadaBuys | 80,000+ awards/year |
| Quebec | SEAO | 80,000+ tenders/year |
| Ontario | Supply Chain Ontario + bidsandtenders | 40,000+ |
| BC | BC Bid | 10,000+ |
| All provinces | Each runs own portal | 20,000+ combined |
| Municipal | bidsandtenders, city portals | 100,000+ |

## What the data says

- **87.2% of federal contracts go to companies outside the top 100 vendors**
- **67.4% of Quebec tenders receive exactly one bid** — less competition than you think
- **29.3% of federal awards are non-competitive** — relationships matter more than proposals

## Where to start

1. Pick your category and map it to demand (competition statistics: [Public Service Index](https://publicserviceindex.org/competition))
2. Check how many bidders your category draws before spending proposal hours
3. Watch renewal cycles — most service contracts repeat on 2-4 year schedules
4. Start with lower-value competitive opportunities to build a track record
